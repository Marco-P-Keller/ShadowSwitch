Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Summary

Shadow Switch does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (scores, currency, unlocked items, settings) is stored locally on your device using Apple's standard storage. It never leaves your device through us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment information. We only receive confirmation from Apple that a purchase was completed in order to unlock the content.

Challenge codes and sharing

When you tap Share, the app creates an image and a text code on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes. No account is required and nothing is uploaded by the app.

Children

The app contains no ads, no external links to social networks inside gameplay and no data collection, and is suitable for all ages.
