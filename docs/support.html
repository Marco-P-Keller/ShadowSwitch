FAQ

How do I restore my purchases?
Open Settings (gear icon on the home screen) → Restore Purchases.

How do challenge codes work?
After a run tap Share. Your friend opens Daily → Friend Challenge, enters your code and plays the exact same run. Beat the score to unlock the Rival skin.

I lost my progress.
Progress is stored on your device. Purchases can always be restored with the same Apple ID.

Request a refund.
Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
